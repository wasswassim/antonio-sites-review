Personal Data: Born in Abruzzo (near the Roma Region)over 60 years ago, Italian Citizen, married with one son and Resident in Lugano (Switzerland).

Are of Operation: His area is Western & Eastern Europe, Middle East, Far East, Africa and America.

 

After obtaining his Diploma in Ragioneria, (equivalent of  “Certified Chartered Accountant”) and attending the Faculty of Economy in Roma’s University and several specialized courses of Doctorate Level in Paris, Bruxelles, London, Toronto, New York, Chicago and Montreal, he entered the industrial World by establishing Industrialunits with Export-Oriented Products.

 

Special Achievements - successfull State Competition to promote the Italian Products Oversea. In order to export his company’s products, in the year 1964 he obtained a Special ITALIAN MINISTRY OF FOREIGN TRADE, assignment for export trade development.

 

Having passed an extensive examination on 25 countries of this Government’s Competition, Mr ANTHONY CANDELORO was classified FIRST on 21countries and SECOND in 4 countries on 25 states competition.

 

The countries on which MR. A. CANDELORO was FIRST classified are: Afghanistan, Argentina, Australia (New South Wales), Brasile(Stato di San Paolo), Camerun, Costa d’ Avorio, Etiopia, Federazione Malese, Giamaica, Giordania, Guinea, Indonesia, Marocco, Messico, Pakistan, Siria, Sud Africa (Provincia del Capo), Sudan, trinidad e Tobago, Uruguay, venezuela.

 

SECOND classified:  Canada (Provincia dell’ Ontario), Formosa, Giappone, Stati Uniti d’America (stato dell’ Illinois). (See the enclosed copy of the Publication of the “Official gazette” of Italian republic Government Nr. 206 of the 24th August 1964 showing the above competition and also the Endorsments from Italian Embassies).

 

Management Appointments: in 1965, he was appointed General Manager of “Italian-North American Trade Center” having branches in Canada.

In 1967 he was charged to Establish the “European Trade center” with 12 Vertical Exhibits promoting the European Product Abroad.

In1969, Mr A. Candeloro established successfully the Pilot Project of 5 Continental. Exhibits’ Complex, putting in operation the FIRST “Permanent International Trade Center” covering European , American, Asian, Australian & African Products.

 

Few years later, he was appionted at the WORLD TRADE CENTER of New York as Senior Consultant of the Special Program Division to develop the Global Marketing “MARKET-MATCH” Program through the “WORLD-MART EXHIBITION HALL”, managed by Mr Anthony Candeloro over one decade at the WTC of New york, with 27 countries affiliation.

 

United Nations Consultancy: Since 1982 after Successful Evaluation, was registered in the “Roster” as Senior International Trage Development Consultant by the International Center of United Nations of Geneva.

 

Intergated Industrial Projects: Up to these days Mr a. Candeloro has been very active in developing Special Projectsin Emerging Countries with Special Assignment to increase the Expansion of the Industry’s trade and the consequent Export Promotional Programs  of several Countries’ Priority Integrated Project including:

 

To assist the estabilshment of the MILAN WORLD TRADE CENTER with the CONFEDERATION OF ITALIAN INDUSTRIES, “CONFINDUSTRIA”.

 

planning and Development of Marine-transportation & Integrated Shipyard-Shipbuilding-Repair and Maintenance Services.

 

Ship’s Management including Fishing-Cargo-Passenger  Yessels.

 

Development of small-scale Industries in the sector of AGRO-BASED PRODUCTION, CONSTRUCTION with low-cost Prefabricated factory-made Structures and Housing.

 

Mr A. Candeloro’s particular interest has been devoted also to the Burter-Counter Trade and Project Financing to develop Projects in developing countries.

 

Furthermore, among several Mr A. Candeloro’s major involvements, there has been the Formation, Implementation-Supervision and Promotion of major International Joint-Ventures and INTERNATIONAL FRANCHISING PROGRAMS.
